# Changelog

All notable changes to FlowFieldSpectra.jl are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Grids are [FlowGeometries.jl](https://github.com/jbphyswx/FlowGeometries.jl) grids, and the coordinate
  system is the grid's geometry type (`CartesianGeometry`, `SphericalGeometry`, `SpheroidGeometry`).
  Structured (uniform and stretched), unstructured, curvilinear and spherical pixelization grids (HEALPix,
  reduced/octahedral Gaussian rings, cubed-sphere, icosahedral, Yin-Yang, Fibonacci) are supported.
- Two backend axes that compose freely: `transform=` (`AutoSpectralBackend`, `DirectSumSpectralBackend`,
  `FFTSpectralBackend`, the NUFFT tags `FlowTransformBindings.FINUFFTBackend()` and
  `FlowTransformBindings.NonuniformFFTsBackend()`, `FSHTSpectralBackend`, `NUFSHTSpectralBackend`) and
  `execution=` (`SerialBackend`, `ThreadedBackend`, `GPUBackend`, `DistributedBackend`, `MPIBackend`,
  `AutoBackend`).
- GPU transforms on any KernelAbstractions device: the FFT through `AbstractFFTs` (CUFFT, rocFFT, FFTW on
  `KA.CPU()`), the hybrid FFT/NUFFT composite, the NUFFT through FlowTransformBindings
  (`NonuniformFFTsBackend()` on any device, `FINUFFTBackend()` as cuFINUFFT on CUDA), a spherical harmonic
  transform (φ-DFT and θ-Legendre contraction), a device-resident NUFSHT, and portable direct-sum kernels
  for the forward and the synthesis on Cartesian and spherical grids.
- Distributed and MPI execution: point-partitionable transforms (DirectSum, NUFFT, NUFSHT projection)
  split the points and sum the α-weighted partial coefficients; the others split the batch axis.
  `MPIBackend(GPUBackend(dev))` runs on a multi-GPU cluster.
- Reusable plans for every transform and execution backend, over a trailing `batch=` shape and
  allocation-free in steady state on `SerialBackend`: `plan_spectrum` with
  `calculate_spectrum!(coeffs, plan, field)`, and `plan_synthesis` with `synthesize!(out, plan, coeffs)`.
  A plan runs on the execution backend it was built for: a direct-sum plan's threaded runs under
  `ThreadedBackend`, a FastSphericalHarmonics plan at one thread under `SerialBackend` and at
  `Threads.nthreads()` under `ThreadedBackend`, the hybrid FFT/NUFFT and direct-sum plans on the device
  under `GPUBackend`. A plan reports its output through `coefficient_size`, `coefficient_type`,
  `wavenumbers` and `allocate_coefficients` (`field_size`, `field_type` and `allocate_field` for
  synthesis), and `close!` releases its NUFFT and NUFSHT library plans.
- `synthesize` on Cartesian and spherical grids: the adjoint of `calculate_spectrum` under the grid's
  quadrature, and its inverse where that quadrature is exact. `FFTSpectralBackend()` synthesizes on a
  grid uniform in some directions and stretched in others through the hybrid composite's inverse: a
  backward FFT over the uniform axes and a type-2 NUFFT along each stretched one, one-shot and through
  `plan_synthesis`, on the host and on a device.
- `plan_spectrum` and `plan_synthesis` with `FFTSpectralBackend()` take `measure`: FFTW's `MEASURE`
  planning by default, `ESTIMATE` with `measure = false`. Every one-shot call, the grid form of
  `calculate_spectrum!` included, plans with `ESTIMATE`.
- Preprocessing through `calculate_spectrum(...; preprocess = Preprocess(...))` and the exported
  `preprocess_field`: typed windows (`Hann`, `Hamming`, `Blackman`, `Tukey`, `NoWindow`) scaled to unit
  mean square under the grid measure, least-squares detrends (`Demean`, `LinearDetrend`, `NoDetrend`)
  under the transform's quadrature on Cartesian and spherical grids, and zero padding (`pad`) of Cartesian
  grids.
- Typed normalization conventions in `SpectralConvention`: `OneSided`/`TwoSided`,
  `DensityScaling`/`PowerScaling`, and the radial-bin estimators `ShellSum`/`ModeAverage`.
- Spheroid grids (`SpheroidGeometry`) take the surface spherical-harmonic expansion over each node's
  geocentric direction, with the grid's area measure as quadrature.
- `inactive` on every spherical transform, host and device, quadrature and `solve = true` fit, says how
  a masked grid's inactive cells enter: `ZeroFill()` (the default) transforms the field extended by zero
  over the whole grid, and `Exclude()` the active cells as their own point set, its quadrature weights
  rescaled to the whole grid's total.
- Estimators: `cross_spectrum`/`cospectrum`/`quadspectrum`, `anisotropic_spectrum` `E(k,θ)`,
  `spectral_vorticity`/`spectral_divergence`, `compensate`/`band_energy`, `welch_power_spectrum`,
  `coherence_spectrum`, `dpss` multitaper tapers, and `lomb_scargle`.
- `physical_wavenumbers` and `SphericalKernels` (cached Legendre tables), shared by every backend.
- Documenter example pages, Internals and API docs, the README gallery (generated by
  `docs/generate_assets/generate_assets.jl`), and CI, Docs, CompatHelper and TagBot workflows.

### Changed
- `transform` defaults to `AutoSpectralBackend()`: the fastest loaded transform whose preconditions the
  grid meets (FFT, the hybrid FFT/NUFFT composite, NUFFT, FastSphericalHarmonics, NUFSHT), and the direct
  sum, with a one-time warning, when none is loaded. The grid form of `calculate_spectrum!` resolves it
  the same way.
- `AutoBackend` resolves to `ThreadedBackend` or `SerialBackend` from the loaded extensions; GPU,
  Distributed and MPI execution need an explicit backend.
- The `transform=` and `execution=` keywords replace the single `backend` argument, and the
  coordinate-range heuristic is removed.
- A real field's Cartesian coefficients are the rfft-packed half of its coefficient box on every
  backend; `FlowTransformBindings.unpacked`/`unpacked!` expand it. The box is `ms`, except on an even axis
  whose samples tell `+m/2` from `-m/2` apart (a stretched axis, a point set, an axis transformed below
  its own length), which it widens to the `m + 1` modes `-m/2 … m/2`. It holds the transform's modes and
  their conjugate mirror, and zero at a mode in neither.
- A real field's spherical coefficients are a real array (`sph_coeff_type`); a complex field's are
  complex.
- Unsupported `(transform, execution, grid)` combinations raise an `ArgumentError`.
- `transect_spectrum!` takes `(E_reduced, ks_phys, coeffs, dims)` and allocates nothing.
- The NUFFT default tolerance follows the element type; `Float32` is supported end to end.
- The GPU spherical transform runs one thread per output mode, with no atomics.
- Minimum Julia is 1.11, for the Project.toml `[sources]`/`[workspace]` tables that pull in the
  unregistered NUFSHT.jl.

### Fixed
- `synthesize` on a real field allocates nothing per mode and point.
- `transect_spectrum` keeping a halved axis weights each stored mode by its `fold_weight`, so `Σ E / ∏dk`
  is the folded Parseval total whichever axes are kept.
- Spherical transforms of a complex field: the direct sums carry the `exp(+imλ)` partner transform, and
  FastSphericalHarmonics, the device SHT and NUFSHT transform one real component at a time.
- A direction's Fourier length is read from its periodic flag, so a non-periodic direction of a node cloud
  ignores a stored `period`.
- `NUFSHTSpectralBackend()` returns the quadrature projection `Σⱼ wⱼ fⱼ Y_lm(xⱼ)`, as every other
  spherical backend does.
- `NUFSHTSpectralBackend()` reads no masked value under `solve = true`; a masked `NaN` made every
  coefficient `NaN`.
- A real field's synthesis, its radial spectrum with no cutoff and its transects sum the whole
  coefficient box on every transform, on a grid whose samples tell `±m/2` apart included; the synthesis
  of a uniform grid transformed below its own length mirrored the stored modes.
- NUFSHT reads a field's batch shape from `ndims(grid)` and writes synthesis in the grid's spatial shape.
- The NUFFT one-shots and the Distributed/MPI point partition accept a curvilinear grid's
  multi-dimensional field.
- A plan constructor that fails releases the NUFFT library plans it built.

### Tested
- Parseval and DirectSum-vs-FFT parity at `D = 1, 2, 3`; plan reuse and batched axes; grid-dispatch
  errors; ExplicitImports and Aqua; `Float32`; rfft-vs-full-FFT equivalence.
- Every transform's widened coefficient box against an inline naive sum on stretched grids and point
  sets, zero at the modes outside the computed ones and their mirror; every synthesis against an inline
  evaluation of the real series over the box; transects and the uncut radial spectrum against the box's
  total.
- Execution-axis parity (Serial, Threaded, Auto, `GPUBackend(KA.CPU())`) for every transform; Distributed
  (in-process workers) and MPI (2 ranks under `mpiexec`) parity against serial; an allocation suite
  asserting zero for every in-place and plan path.
- The device-generic paths run on CI through `GPUBackend(KA.CPU())`, and the direct-sum plans and
  synthesis on a JLArrays device that refuses host scalar indexing; `gpu/gpu_parity.jl` checks the CUDA
  paths (CUFFT, cuFINUFFT) on CUDA hardware.
